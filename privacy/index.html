Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Arpy (“the app”) is developed by DigiNova Labs. We designed Arpy so that your network data stays with you.

Data that stays on your device

Network scans, device lists, names and notes you add, scan history and reports are created and stored only on your device. We do not operate servers that receive this data and we do not require an account.

Network services the app contacts

  • Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com) and ipinfo.io — to show your public IP address and internet provider and to run the speed test.
  • rdap.org — when you run a Whois lookup.
  • The DNS servers you choose in the DNS tools.

These requests contain your public IP address, as with any internet request, and are not linked to an identity by us.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google’s consent message, and you can change your choices any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. Ads are limited to general-audience (G-rated) content. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Arpy Pro subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Permissions

  • Local network — required to discover devices on your network.
  • Location (optional) — iOS and macOS require it to read the name of the Wi‑Fi network. Arpy never stores or shares your location.
  • Notifications (optional) — for new device alerts.

Children

Arpy is a general-audience utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

You can delete all app data at any time in Settings › Clear all data, or by deleting the app. For questions or requests contact support@arpy.tr.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
